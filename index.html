About the App

Animal Sketch: Draw & Color is a drawing and coloring app. The app lets users draw, color, save, and share artwork where supported. No account or login is required.

Information We Collect

Animal Sketch: Draw & Color does not ask users to provide their name, phone number, email address, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

The app may store drawings, coloring progress, or exported images locally on the user’s device. This content is not uploaded to a READY COM server by the app.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this information only to respond to support, privacy, or app-related requests.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Animal Sketch: Draw & Color uses third-party advertising services to show ads and measure ad performance.

Advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device model, operating system, network information, app interactions, ad views, ad clicks, diagnostics, and crash-related data.

This information may be used to deliver ads, personalize ads where allowed, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud, and keep advertising services secure.

The app may use Unity LevelPlay / ironSource mediation and related advertising partners. Their privacy policies can be found here:

  • Unity / LevelPlay / ironSource: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

Sharing and Saving Content

When you save or share artwork, the content may be stored on your device or shared through another app selected by you. The receiving app or service handles shared content according to its own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app may use storage or media-related access only to save, open, or share drawings where supported by the device and Android version.

Internet access is used for advertising and related services.

Advertising ID access may be used by advertising SDKs for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and frequency capping.

Children’s Privacy

Animal Sketch: Draw & Color is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Data Retention and Deletion

Artwork and app data stored locally remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears app data, or uninstalls the app.

Advertising partners manage the retention of information they collect according to their own privacy policies.

Users may clear app data or uninstall the app from Android settings.

Your Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may be able to reset or delete your advertising ID or change ad personalization settings in Android settings.

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information that READY COM may hold from support communications.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.
