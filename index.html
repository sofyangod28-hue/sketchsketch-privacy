Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how sketchDraw handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

sketchDraw does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information.

The app may access photos or the camera only when you choose to start a project from the gallery or take a photo. The selected image is used to create or edit your drawing.

How Your Drawings Are Stored

Drawings and temporary image files are stored locally on your device in the app storage area. They are not uploaded by the app to a server.

Sharing

If you use the share feature, Android lets you choose another app to receive your drawing. The selected receiving app handles the shared image according to its own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

This version of sketchDraw does not include advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or user account services.

Permissions

  • Camera permission is used only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Photo or storage access is used only when you choose an image from your device.
  • Vibration may be used for small interaction feedback.

Children's Privacy

sketchDraw is a general drawing app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The updated version will be shown in the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the app developer through the support channel listed on the app store page.
